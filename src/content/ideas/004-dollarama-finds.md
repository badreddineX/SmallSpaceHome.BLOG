Not everything at Dollarama reads as cheap. A few things genuinely pass for more:

- **Plain woven or felt baskets** ($3–$4.50) — decant your open shelving into matching baskets and the whole unit goes from cluttered to considered. Consistency is what sells it.
- **Clear acrylic drawer bins** ($2–$4) — for the bathroom or a kitchen drawer. They look like the $12 versions once they're full and lined up.
- **A single stem or eucalyptus sprig** ($1.50) — one in a plain bottle on a shelf. Skip the full bouquets; those are the ones that look fake.

What to avoid: anything with a printed "farmhouse" slogan, glossy gold plastic, or fake succulents in pots.

Budget: $15. Spend it on baskets first — matching storage is the highest-impact $15 in a small space.
