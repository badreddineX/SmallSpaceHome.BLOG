WEEKLY NEWSLETTER IDEAS
======================

One .md file per weekly email. The broadcast job (api/broadcast.js) sends the
lowest `order:` number that hasn't gone out yet, one per week. Sent ideas are
never repeated (tracked by filename in the `issues` table).

Only .md files are picked up — this .txt is ignored.

FORMAT
------
---
title: "The $14 under-bed fix that clears a whole closet"
order: 1
price: "$14 CAD"                                        # optional — badge
image: "/images/baskets-laundry-by-bed.jpg"             # optional — thumb variant auto-used
relatedPost: "under-bed-storage-ideas-small-apartment"  # optional — "The full guide →" link
---

Body = the tip. 120-170 words, one concrete thing to do this week. Plain text.
**Bold** and [links](https://smallspacehome.ca) work. Blank line between paragraphs.

QUEUE
-----
1  001-under-bed-bags        Storage
2  002-over-door-organizers  Organization
3  003-command-strip-walls   Decor
4  004-dollarama-finds       Budget Tips

Add the next as 005-... To reorder, change the `order:` numbers.
