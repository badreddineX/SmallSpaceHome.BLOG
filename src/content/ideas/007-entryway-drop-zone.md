Most small apartments have no entryway — you walk straight into the living room, and keys, mail, shoes, and bags pile up wherever you drop them.

Build a drop zone on the first metre of wall this week, no drilling:

- **A slim console or a narrow shelf** ($30–$50, IKEA Canada or Kijiji) — even 20 cm deep is enough for a key dish, mail tray, and a lamp.
- **Adhesive hooks** ($8) for two coats and a bag. Command's larger hooks hold a winter coat fine if you follow the weight rating.
- **A shoe tray** ($10, Canadian Tire) so wet boots have a home and aren't tracked across the floor.

The point isn't storage volume — it's giving the daily stuff a designated spot so it stops migrating into the room.
