The single fastest way to make a rental living room feel less like a rental is to stop using the overhead light in the evening. One bright bulb from the ceiling flattens everything.

This week, add **two or three low, warm light sources** and switch the big light off after dark:

- A floor lamp in the corner ($40–$60, IKEA Canada) — arched or a simple column.
- A table lamp on the TV console or a side table ($25–$40, HomeSense).
- Something small and low — a lamp on a stack of books, or string lights along a shelf ($15, Amazon.ca).

Put them on a smart plug or a cheap remote outlet ($10) so one click runs the set. Swap every bulb to **2700K "warm white"** ($4 each) — "daylight" bulbs are what make a room feel like an office.
