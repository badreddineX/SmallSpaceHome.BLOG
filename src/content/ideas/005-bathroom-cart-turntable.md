Most rental bathrooms give you a mirror, a sink with no cabinet, and nowhere to put anything. Two cheap pieces fix that this week:

- **A narrow rolling cart** (~$20–$25 at IKEA Canada — the RÅSKOG, or Amazon.ca dupes) that slides into the gap beside the toilet or under a pedestal sink. Three tiers holds towels, backups, and the stuff that usually lives on the back of the toilet.
- **A turntable inside the vanity or on a shelf** ($4–$8 at Dollarama or Canadian Tire). Spin it instead of digging. One for skincare, one for cleaning sprays.

Add a tension rod inside the shower for a second hanging row if you're really tight ($6).

Total under $35, nothing drilled, all of it comes with you when you move.
