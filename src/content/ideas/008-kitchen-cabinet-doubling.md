Small kitchen cabinets waste most of their height — one shelf, then a foot of dead air above the plates. Three cheap add-ons reclaim it:

- **Shelf risers** ($6–$10 each, Dollarama or Amazon.ca) — a second level for mugs or tins inside an existing shelf.
- **Under-shelf baskets** that clip onto the shelf above ($8) — instant hidden drawer for tea, foil, sandwich bags.
- **Over-the-door racks** on the inside of the cabinet door ($15–$20) — spices, wraps, cutting boards, all off the shelf entirely.

Do the cabinet you open most first. You'll usually find you can empty a whole shelf onto the risers and baskets, which frees space for the stuff currently stacked on the counter.
