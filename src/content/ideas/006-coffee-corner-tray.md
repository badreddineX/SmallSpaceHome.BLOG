A dedicated coffee spot makes a small kitchen feel considered instead of cramped — and it keeps the machine, mugs, and beans from spreading across the whole counter.

This week, corral it onto **one tray** (~$15, Simons or HomeSense). Everything that lives on the tray is the coffee station; everything else goes in a cupboard.

- Machine or press on the tray, plus a small canister for beans or pods ($8, Canadian Tire).
- **One shelf or rail above it** for four mugs — a stick-on shelf ($15) or an over-cabinet hook rail so the mugs aren't taking drawer space.
- A little dish for spoons, one plant or a framed print behind it for warmth.

The tray is the trick: it gives the corner an edge, so it reads as a station, not clutter.
