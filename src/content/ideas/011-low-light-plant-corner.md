A green corner warms up a small space more than almost any decor object — but most apartment plants die because the light is wrong, not because you forgot to water them.

This week, build one corner around **low-light plants that actually cope**:

- **Snake plant, ZZ plant, pothos, or a cast iron plant** ($10–$20 each at IKEA Canada, Canadian Tire, or a grocery store) — all tolerate a few feet back from a window.
- Group **three at different heights** — one on the floor, one on a stool or stack of books, one trailing from a shelf. Odd numbers and varied heights read as intentional.
- Skip anything labelled "bright indirect light" unless the spot is right at the glass. That label kills more apartment plants than neglect.

Water on a fixed day (Sunday) so it becomes a habit, not a guess.
