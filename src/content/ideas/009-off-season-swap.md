If your closet holds both your winter parka and your summer clothes year-round, roughly a third of it is storing things you can't use for six months.

This week, pull everything you won't wear for the next season and move it out of the daily closet:

- **Two large zip totes or vacuum bags** ($20–$30, Canadian Tire) — parka, sweaters, boots into one; the bag flattens to nothing.
- Store them **on the top shelf, under the bed, or on top of the wardrobe** — the hard-to-reach spots are perfect for stuff you touch twice a year.
- Label each bag with the season and a rough list so you're not opening all of them in October.

Do it every equinox. The closet you actually use gets noticeably roomier, and nothing new needs buying after the first set of bags.
