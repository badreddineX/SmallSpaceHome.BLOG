The reason most rentals stay bare is fear of the damage deposit. Here's what genuinely peels off clean, tested on painted drywall:

- **Command picture strips** (~$10 CAD/pack) — hold a framed print up to about 1.5 kg. Press for 30 seconds, wait an hour before hanging.
- **Command hooks** for a mirror, hats, a hanging plant, string lights.
- **Removable peel-and-stick wallpaper** on *one* accent wall or the back of a bookshelf — not the whole room.

Two rules keep your deposit safe: **don't exceed the weight rating**, and **pull the tab straight down, slowly** — never yank it off the wall. Skip putty and nails entirely.

Do one wall this week. A single piece of art at eye level changes a room more than a whole gallery does.
