New apartment furniture in Canada is priced for houses. Second-hand solid wood is often cheaper than new flatpack and holds up far better.

This week, find **one piece** on Facebook Marketplace or Kijiji — a real dresser, a solid coffee table, a bookshelf:

- Search by **neighbourhood pickup radius**, sort by newest, and set an alert for the item you want. Good pieces go in hours.
- Filter your budget to **half of new retail** — a $500-new sideboard should be $150–$200 used.
- Message with a specific pickup time, not "is this available?" Sellers ignore the vague ones.
- Check drawers slide, legs are solid, and there's no musty smell (that doesn't come out).

One good used piece changes a room more than three cheap new ones — and you can usually resell it for what you paid when you move.
