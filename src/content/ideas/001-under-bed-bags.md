The gap under your bed is usually the single biggest chunk of unused storage in a small apartment — and most people waste it on dust.

This week: buy one set of **IKEA SKUBB under-bed bags** (~$14 CAD for two). Pull everything you only touch twice a year — winter coats in summer, summer clothes in winter, spare bedding, that box of cables — and move it under the bed. Zip the bags shut so nothing gets dusty.

If your frame sits low, $20 of bed risers buys you another 12 cm of clearance and turns a tight fit into an easy one.

The point isn't the bags. It's that once the seasonal stuff is under the bed, your actual closet suddenly has room for the clothes you wear now — no purge required.
