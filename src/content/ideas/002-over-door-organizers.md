Every interior door in your apartment has a full metre of vertical storage hanging off the back of it, doing nothing.

This week: pick your two worst-clutter rooms and add an **over-the-door organizer** to each (~$14–$20 CAD each, no tools, hooks right over the top of the door).

- **Bathroom door** — the pocket kind for skincare, hair tools, and the backups that never fit in the cabinet.
- **Entry closet door** — a shoe organizer, but for gloves, sunscreen, reusable bags, and dog stuff.
- **Bedroom door** — a slim hook rail for tomorrow's outfit or the coat you actually wore today.

Renter-safe, comes off in one second when you move, and it clears the exact horizontal surfaces that always end up as dumping grounds.
