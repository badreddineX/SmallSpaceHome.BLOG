The bedroom in a small Canadian apartment almost always has the same problem: the closet is too small, there is no storage dresser because there is no room for one, and the floor under the bed is either wasted or filled with mismatched bins that slide around and collect dust.

A platform bed with built-in storage solves all three issues at once. It converts dead under-bed space into organized storage, keeps the bedroom floor clear, and eliminates the need for a freestanding dresser in many apartments. This guide covers what is actually available in Canada in 2026, what each type holds, and how to choose.

---

## Two Types of Storage Platform Beds

### Drawer beds

Pull-out drawers are built into one or both sides of the platform base. Most queen beds come with 2 drawers (one per side); some models offer 4. The drawers typically do not have full extension, meaning they pull out about 60–70% of their depth before stopping — planned storage works better than ad hoc use.

**What fits:** Folded clothing, bedding, seasonal items, shoes (lying flat), craft supplies, extra towels. Drawers work well for anything you access 1–3 times a week.

**What does not fit:** Suitcases, large duvets, bulky winter gear. The drawer height is typically 18–22 cm — a rolling suitcase does not fit.

**Clearance:** Drawer beds need about 60 cm of free space on each side of the bed for the drawers to open fully. In a small bedroom, this is often the limiting factor.

### Hydraulic lift beds

The entire platform (with mattress) lifts up on gas struts, revealing a large open cavity that runs the full length and width of the bed. There are no side clearance requirements — the storage is accessed from the foot or side by lifting the mattress.

**What fits:** Everything a drawer bed cannot. Full suitcases, large duvets and comforters still in their storage bags, seasonal clothing in bins, athletic gear, extra bedding sets, books, electronics in boxes.

**Capacity:** A queen lift bed holds approximately 400–600 litres. That is the equivalent of 3–4 large IKEA SAMLA bins (55 L each) laid flat, or a full wardrobe of off-season clothing plus a suitcase.

**Trade-off:** You must get off the mattress to access storage. The mechanism is strong — gas struts hold the platform fully open with no effort — but it is not a quick grab like a drawer. Best for items you access monthly, not daily.

## Platform Storage Beds Available in Canada

### IKEA SLATTUM (drawer bed)

**Price:** ~$350–$450 CAD (queen)
**Storage:** 2 pull-out drawers, one per side, no lid — open top storage
**Height:** 43 cm from floor to top of frame

The SLATTUM is IKEA's entry-level upholstered drawer bed. The drawers are open-top (no drawer front cover), so they work as open bins rather than closed drawers — fine for folded bedding, less good for loose small items. Frame is fabric-upholstered over engineered wood. Available in a few neutral colours. Ships to most Canadian addresses, available for pickup or delivery at IKEA Canada locations.

Best for: Budget-conscious renters who primarily need off-season bedding and clothing storage.

### IKEA NORDLI (drawer bed)

**Price:** ~$500–$700 CAD (queen, depends on configuration)
**Storage:** Modular — 2, 4, or 6 drawers depending on configuration
**Height:** 38 cm

The NORDLI is IKEA's most flexible storage bed. The modular system lets you configure more drawers on one side than the other, add a headboard with charging ports, and customize width. White only (as of 2026). Drawers are covered with the same white finish — cleaner look than the SLATTUM. The soft-close mechanism on higher-end configurations is good but not as smooth as purpose-built bedroom furniture.

Best for: Maximising drawer storage in a studio or one-bedroom apartment where the drawer configuration can be matched to available room layout.

### IKEA BRIMNES (drawer bed)

**Price:** ~$450–$550 CAD (queen)
**Storage:** 4 drawers (2 per side), closed-front
**Height:** 47 cm

The BRIMNES is IKEA's four-drawer option. Closed-front drawers look like proper furniture rather than open bins. The headboard has two small shelves built in, adding nightstand-equivalent storage. White or black finish. One of the most widely owned beds in Canadian apartments due to the four-drawer layout — doubles the storage of most drawer beds at nearly the same price point.

Best for: Anyone who wants to use the storage bed as a dresser replacement, eliminating the need for a separate chest of drawers in a small bedroom.

### Zinus Upholstered Platform Bed with Storage (drawer bed)

**Price:** ~$400–$600 CAD (queen, Amazon.ca)
**Storage:** 2 or 4 drawers depending on model
**Height:** 45–50 cm

Zinus is one of the most reviewed mattress and bed frame brands on Amazon.ca. Their storage beds are available in queen and king, upholstered in light grey, beige, or dark grey fabric. Assembly is straightforward. The drawers are side-slide with a stopper (no full extension). Build quality is good for the price — the frame is heavy engineered wood over a steel grid, which is more rigid than pure MDF construction.

Best for: Renters who want a complete packaged look (upholstered, no visible wood grain) without IKEA's assembly complexity.

### Wayfair Canada: Hydraulic Lift Options

Wayfair Canada carries the largest selection of hydraulic lift beds available to Canadian buyers. Price range for a queen lift bed: **$600–$1,200 CAD**, with the most common models landing around $700–$900.

**What to look for on Wayfair:**

- **Gas strut rating**: Should be listed — look for 1,500 N or higher. Lower-rated struts struggle with a full mattress.
- **Base material**: Solid wood slats or a steel grid hold the mattress better than thin MDF panels, which can bow over time.
- **Access point**: Foot-of-bed lift vs. side lift. Foot lift requires more floor space at the end of the bed (50+ cm) but does not require clearance on the sides.

**Notable models:**

- **Lark Manor Alayah Upholstered Storage Bed** (~$750–$900, queen): Consistent reviews on Wayfair Canada. Linen upholstery, hydraulic lift from the foot end, good cavity depth for suitcases.
- **Winston Porter and George Oliver brands** on Wayfair: Frequently on sale. Build quality ranges widely — read the 1-star reviews specifically for frame cracking or strut failure reports before buying.

Best for: Renters who need maximum storage capacity and are willing to plan access (not grabbing items daily) in exchange for large volume.

### Amazon.ca: Molblly and Giantex Lift Beds

Two brands consistently available on Amazon.ca with good shipping to major Canadian cities:

**Molblly Storage Platform Bed** (~$500–$700, queen): Hydraulic lift, upholstered in grey or beige fabric, free shipping to most provinces. Reviews are strong for the gas strut durability specifically — a common failure point on cheaper lift beds.

**Giantex Platform Bed with Storage** (~$400–$600, queen): Budget lift bed with solid reviews. Assembly instructions are less clear than Zinus or IKEA equivalents, so budget extra time. Ships from Canadian warehouses in most cases — important for avoiding duties.

## What to Measure Before Buying

### Bedroom clearance

| Bed type | Side clearance needed | End clearance needed |
|---|---|---|
| Drawer bed (2-drawer) | 60 cm per drawer side | 60 cm minimum |
| Drawer bed (4-drawer) | 60 cm both sides | 60 cm minimum |
| Hydraulic lift (foot-lift) | 50 cm both sides | 80+ cm |
| Hydraulic lift (side-lift) | 80+ cm one side | 50 cm minimum |

In a small apartment bedroom (typically 3 × 3.5 m or 3 × 4 m), a 4-drawer queen bed often cannot open all four drawers — the side with the closet or wall does not have 60 cm. Check your actual clearance before choosing a 4-drawer over 2-drawer.

### Ceiling and loft clearance

Platform storage beds sit higher than basic frames. A hydraulic lift bed with a 30 cm mattress on a 50 cm base sits about 80 cm off the floor — not unusual, but something to verify in apartments with low-profile furniture or low ceilings.

### Door width

All storage beds ship disassembled. The longest single piece in a queen storage bed frame is typically 215 cm (the side rail). Standard Canadian apartment exterior doors are 81 cm wide; interior doors are 76–81 cm. A 215 cm rail at any angle passes through an 81 cm doorway — no issue.

## Storage Bed vs. Separate Under-Bed Storage

If the bed you have is already good and replacing it is not practical, standalone under-bed storage is the alternative.

**Under-bed storage bins** (IKEA SKUBB or similar, ~$15–$30 for 2): Work on beds with 20+ cm of floor clearance. Flat bins for clothing and bedding; rolling bins for heavier items. The problem is access — bins slide to the back and are inconvenient to retrieve from.

**Under-bed storage drawers on wheels** (Amazon.ca, ~$40–$80): Freestanding rolling drawers that live under a platform bed with adequate clearance. Easier access than flat bins. Limited capacity — typically 2 drawers, each 15 cm deep.

**Storage bed verdict:** If you plan to stay in the apartment 12+ months and the bedroom has adequate clearance, a storage bed pays for itself in cabinet space freed. If you move frequently or the bedroom is very narrow, bins under the current bed are the practical choice.
