Laundry storage gets ignored until you're standing in a closet the size of a phone booth, trying to find detergent balanced on top of the dryer. In-suite stacked washer/dryer units are now standard in a lot of newer Canadian condo and apartment buildings, and the closet around them is almost always an afterthought. I've organized three of these tight closets in three different Toronto rentals, and the fixes are cheap, renter-safe, and take under an hour.

This guide covers both situations: the in-suite stacked closet, and the shared building laundry room down the hall. Neither needs drilling, and neither needs much money.

## How Do You Organize a Tight In-Suite Laundry Closet?

Most stacked washer/dryer closets in Canadian apartments are under 3 feet wide, with nothing above the machines but dead air. Statistics Canada's most recent housing data shows in-suite laundry is now included in roughly a third of newer purpose-built rental units ([CMHC Rental Market Survey](https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/), 2024), yet almost none come with built-in storage.

The fix is to treat the space above the machines as your primary storage zone, since the floor space around a stacked unit is usually zero. A tension-rod shelf braces between the closet's side walls and sits just above the dryer vent clearance, holding detergent, dryer sheets, and a mesh bag for delicates.

**What to buy:** Amazon.ca tension-rod laundry shelf (~$35–$50 CAD), sized to your closet width before ordering — most stacked closets run 27–30 inches wide.

### Wire Shelving as a No-Drill Alternative

If a tension shelf won't fit your closet's exact width, a freestanding wire shelf unit that sits flush beside the dryer works just as well. Canadian Tire and HomeSense both carry narrow wire units (12–16 inches deep) built for tight laundry nooks.

**Cost:** Canadian Tire narrow wire shelving unit, ~$40–$60 CAD.

## What Goes on an Over-the-Door Detergent Rack?

An over-door rack turns the back of your laundry closet door into a second storage zone, holding detergent pods, stain sticks, and lint roller refills at eye level. It clips over a standard door in under two minutes with zero tools or drilling required, and at $22–$32 CAD it's one of the fastest, cheapest storage upgrades available for a rental laundry closet.

Keep the heaviest items, full detergent jugs, on the lowest shelf so the rack doesn't sag or pull the door hinge over time. In my current apartment, moving detergent off the dryer top and onto a door rack freed up the only flat surface in the closet, which now holds my drying rack when it's folded down.

**What to buy:** Amazon.ca over-door laundry organizer, ~$22–$32 CAD. Look for one rated for at least 15 lbs if you're storing liquid detergent.

## Should You Add a Rolling Cart Beside the Laundry Closet?

A rolling cart parked beside your stacked unit solves the "no floor space" problem by adding storage that moves out of the way when you need to open the dryer door fully. The IKEA RASKOG cart runs about $39 CAD and measures roughly 18 inches wide, narrow enough to slide into the gap beside most stacked units without blocking the dryer door. It holds a hamper, folded towels, or a stain-treatment kit on wheels.

The IKEA RASKOG cart (~$39 CAD) is the standard pick here, same one recommended in our <a href="/blog/storage-ideas-for-small-places">small apartment storage ideas</a> guide, because its narrow footprint (about 18 inches wide) slides into the gap next to most stacked units. Roll it out to sort laundry, roll it back when you're done.

**Cost:** IKEA RASKOG 3-tier cart, ~$39 CAD.

### A Slim Hamper That Doubles as a Cart

If a full cart is too much for your space, a slim rolling hamper with a pull-out laundry bag does a smaller version of the same job. It holds dirty laundry and rolls straight to the machines without you carrying an armful down the hall.

**Cost:** Amazon.ca rolling laundry hamper, ~$30–$45 CAD.

## How Do You Manage Laundry Without In-Suite Machines?

Renters without in-suite laundry rely on a shared building laundry room, and 68% of apartment residents in a 2023 rental housing survey said they wanted better storage for laundry supplies at home ([Apartment List Renter Survey](https://www.apartmentlist.com/), 2023). A portable caddy solves the biggest pain point: carrying detergent, coins, and dryer sheets down multiple floors in one trip.

A basic plastic caddy from Dollarama (~$4–$6 CAD) has a divided tray and a center handle, built for exactly this. Most renters default to a tote bag for laundry supplies, but a caddy with a rigid divided tray actually stops detergent bottles from tipping over mid-hallway, which a soft bag never does.

**What to buy:** Dollarama plastic cleaning caddy, ~$4–$6 CAD, or a canvas laundry tote from Amazon.ca (~$20–$30 CAD) if you want something sturdier for a longer walk to the laundry room.

### Keep a Quarters Jar Right by the Door

If your building laundry room still runs on coins, a small labelled jar by your front door means you're never digging through a junk drawer on laundry day. I kept a $2 Dollarama jar labelled "laundry" on my entry shelf for two years, and it's the single cheapest fix on this list.

**Cost:** Dollarama glass jar, ~$2 CAD.

## How Do You Sort Laundry in a Small Apartment Without a Dedicated Room?

Sorting laundry without a mudroom or laundry room means using stackable bins or a divided hamper that lives in a closet or bedroom corner. A three-section hamper stand like IKEA's JÄLL costs about $40 CAD and sorts darks, lights, and delicates automatically, turning a weekly chore into a passive habit instead of a pile that needs re-sorting on wash day.

Place the hamper inside a closet on a slim shelf, using the same vertical-storage logic covered in our <a href="/blog/small-apartment-organization">small apartment organization guide</a>, so it doesn't eat floor space in the bedroom itself.

**Cost:** IKEA JÄLL 3-section laundry bag stand, ~$40 CAD.

## FAQ

**How do I organize a small in-suite laundry closet?**
Start above the machines with a tension-mounted shelf. Add a door-back rack and a rolling cart beside the unit if space allows. This combination fits closets under 3 feet wide.

**What is the best storage for stackable washer and dryer units?**
A tension-rod shelf that braces above the stack, no drilling needed. It's the highest-capacity option for the least amount of space, at around $35–$50 CAD.

**How do I carry laundry supplies to a shared building laundry room?**
Use a caddy or tote to carry detergent, dryer sheets, and quarters in one trip. A $4–$6 CAD Dollarama caddy works fine for most people.

**How much does it cost to set up laundry storage in a small apartment?**
A full in-suite system runs $90–$140 CAD. Shared building laundry storage costs under $50 CAD for a caddy and rolling hamper.
