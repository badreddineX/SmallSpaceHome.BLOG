"Which space-saving storage bed should I choose for a small studio apartment?" is a real question with a real answer that depends on one measurement: the clearance under whatever bed you have right now, or plan to buy. Grab a tape measure before reading further — it decides which half of this guide applies to you.

If you already have bins and risers figured out and just need the how-to, [under-bed storage ideas for small apartments](/blog/under-bed-storage-ideas-small-apartment) covers that in more depth. This page is the buy-new-or-work-with-what-you-have decision, one level up.

**TL;DR:** Under 15 cm of clearance and buying a new bed anyway → get a drawer-base storage bed like the IKEA NORDLI ($399–$699 CAD). Already own a decent frame → bed risers ($20–$28 CAD) plus flat bins ($20–$40 CAD) gets most of the benefit for a fraction of the price. Only go storage-bed if you're replacing the bed regardless — buying one purely for storage rarely pencils out.

## Storage Bed vs. Bins and Risers: Quick Comparison

| Option | Cost (CAD) | Capacity | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bed risers only | $20–$28 | Adds 12–15 cm clearance for existing bins | 10 minutes |
| Risers + flat bins (2–4) | $40–$70 | 2–4 bins' worth, bending required to access | 30 minutes |
| Drawer-base storage bed (IKEA NORDLI) | $399–$699 | 8–10 bins' worth, no bending | Half a day (assembly) |
| Platform bed with built-in drawers (other brands) | $450–$900 | Similar to NORDLI, varies by model | Half a day (assembly) |

## When Should You Buy a Storage Bed Instead of Bins?

A storage bed is worth the extra cost specifically when you're furnishing a room from scratch or your current clearance is under 15 cm and can't be fixed with risers — in either case, the bed itself becomes the apartment's primary storage system instead of an add-on to it.

The math: a queen NORDLI runs $399–$699 CAD depending on drawer configuration, versus $40–$70 CAD for the riser-plus-bins version of the same idea. That's a real gap, but it's the wrong comparison if you need a bed anyway — at that point you're not choosing "storage bed vs. no storage," you're choosing "storage bed vs. a plain bed frame plus separate bins," and the price difference shrinks to roughly $300–$500 CAD for triple the capacity and zero visible bins in the room.

If your current bed frame is otherwise fine and you're not planning to replace it, buying a storage bed purely for the drawers rarely makes financial sense. Fix the clearance with risers first.

## How Much Do Bed Risers and Bins Actually Hold?

Bed risers add 12–15 cm of clearance under most existing frames, which is usually enough to fit two rows of flat storage bins per side of a queen or double bed — roughly 2–4 bins total, holding an off-season wardrobe swap or spare bedding.

**Bed risers** ($20–$28 CAD, Amazon.ca or Canadian Tire) slide under each leg and add height without touching the frame itself — reversible in seconds, no tools required. **Flat lidded bins** ($8–$15 CAD each) protect shape-sensitive items like shoes and folded sweaters from dust. **Vacuum-seal bags** ($10–$18 CAD for a multipack) compress bulky items like duvets and winter coats to a quarter of their size, the better option specifically when clearance stays under 15 cm even with risers.

Full breakdown of bins vs. bags vs. what never belongs under a bed is in [under-bed storage ideas for small apartments](/blog/under-bed-storage-ideas-small-apartment).

## What Does a Drawer-Base Storage Bed Actually Hold?

A queen-size IKEA NORDLI with the full drawer base holds roughly 8–10 standard bins' worth of volume, split across 2–4 drawers depending on configuration — typically 2–3 times what risers and bins manage in the same footprint, with the added benefit of drawers you slide rather than bins you crouch to reach.

That capacity difference matters most for a genuinely small studio where the bed is the single largest piece of furniture in the room and effectively has to double as the apartment's main storage system, not just hold spare bedding.

**IKEA NORDLI** — $399–$699 CAD depending on size and drawer count, ships flat-pack, assembles in the room. **Other drawer-base platform frames** (various brands via Amazon.ca or Wayfair Canada) — $450–$900 CAD, similar capacity, worth comparing if NORDLI's specific dimensions don't fit your room.

## Can a Storage Bed Fit Through a Small Apartment's Doors and Elevator?

Drawer-base frames like the NORDLI ship in several flat boxes and assemble inside the room, so getting the pieces in is no harder than any other flat-pack furniture delivery — the real constraint is the assembled frame's weight if you ever need to move it again.

A full queen NORDLI frame weighs roughly 70–90 kg once assembled, noticeably heavier than a basic platform frame, which matters at your next move or lease renewal more than it matters on delivery day. Worth factoring in if you expect to move within a year or two.

## The One-Measurement Decision

Measure your current clearance, or the clearance of the frame you're about to buy, before spending anything. Under 15 cm and not replacing the bed: get risers first, cheapest fix, ten minutes. Under 15 cm and replacing the bed anyway, or furnishing from scratch: go straight to a drawer-base frame like the NORDLI, since the price gap versus a plain frame plus bins is smaller than it looks.
