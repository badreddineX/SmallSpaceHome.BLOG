# Month 1 Posting Schedule — SmallSpace Home

Fully concrete, copy-paste-ready calendar for your first 4 weeks on Instagram
and Pinterest. Every session below has the exact pin file, exact description,
exact link, and exact board/highlight — nothing to decide on the day, just
execute.

**Cadence:** 3 sessions/week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday — starting July 20),
one asset per session, posted to both platforms at the same time. See
`POSTING-CALENDAR.md` for the two-phase frequency plan (Instagram ramps up
to 5-6x/week after this first month).

All three run a parallel 4-week rollout: Pin 1 → Pin 2 → (Story/Feed
fallback, since the short isn't built yet — see note) → Pin 3.

## Weekly check-in reminder

Every **Monday** (before that day's post), spend 10 minutes in Pinterest
Analytics + Instagram Insights checking saves, outbound clicks, and
impressions on last week's posts. After this first 4-week cycle completes,
compare Post A vs B vs C — whichever got more saves/clicks tells you which
category (Storage, Budget Tips, Organization) to lean into harder for
Month 2's post picks.
