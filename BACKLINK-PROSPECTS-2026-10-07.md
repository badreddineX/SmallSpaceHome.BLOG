## 4. Content-Type Analysis: What Earns the Most Links

### CAD (Small-Space Living)
- **Top performer: "Before & After" makeover posts** -- transformation content earns editorial links and roundup mentions from other bloggers who curate them.
- **DIY built-in / hack tutorials** (e.g., IKEA hacks for small spaces) -- these get linked from resource pages and Pinterest-driven roundups.
- **Seasonal "X ideas for small apartments"** listicles -- frequently cited in roundup posts.

### UK (Renter Decor)
- **Top performer: "Renter-friendly" how-to guides** -- solving a specific landlord constraint (no drilling, removable wallpaper) attracts links from other UK lifestyle bloggers facing the same problems.
- **Room reveal / room tour posts** -- UK interiors bloggers frequently link to each other's reveals in editorial features.
- **Budget breakdowns** (full room makeover under a price cap) -- these earn resource-page and roundup links.

### AU (Outdoor / Coastal)
- **Top performer: Outdoor entertaining / styling guides** -- Australian bloggers link heavily to seasonal outdoor entertaining content (especially pre-summer).
- **Native plant / coastal garden content** -- earns editorial links from garden and lifestyle blogs.
- **Product roundups with local (AU) retailers** -- other bloggers link to these as shopping resources because local sourcing info is scarce.
