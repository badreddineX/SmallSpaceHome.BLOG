# Canada — Digital Product Plan

## The Product: First Apartment Checklist & Moving-In Guide

**Who it's for:** First-time renters moving into a small apartment in a Canadian city (Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal) — the exact reader already on smallspacehome.ca.

**What it is:** An 8–10 page PDF/Canva download with:
1. Room-by-room essentials checklist (kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, living room) — checkboxes, not paragraphs
2. **Renter-Safe Setup page** — no-drill solutions (Command strips, tension rods, peel-and-stick backsplash) — the real differentiator vs. generic checklists
3. **Move-in inspection checklist** — protects the damage deposit (photograph rooms, note existing damage, test outlets/appliances)
4. Budget tiers per item (budget/mid/splurge) — CAD pricing, real Canadian retailers named (IKEA Canada, Amazon.ca, Wayfair Canada, HomeSense)

**Price:** $8–$15 base version.

## Why This Product (the evidence)

- A comparable Etsy listing ("First Apartment Checklist Printable") has **4.9★, 2,300 reviews** — real proof of sustained demand, not just category existence.
- Matches content already published on the blog (renter-friendly decor, IKEA hacks, studio apartment posts) — the buyer isn't a stranger, they're already reading about this exact problem.
- Matches 2 of the blog's 4 Pinterest pillars directly (Organization + Storage).

## Competition Reality Check

**Not low-competition** — Etsy's "new home checklist" category has 5,000+ listings, "new home moving checklist" 1,000+. Less saturated than budget spreadsheets (32K–59K) but still real, contested. What the 2,300-review listing proves: a specific, well-differentiated listing can break out inside competition — not that the niche is uncontested.

**Differentiation that matters:**
- Canadian-specific (CAD prices, Canadian retailers) — most competing content is US-focused, a real gap per the blog's own competitor research
- Renter-specific (no-drill, deposit-safe) — addresses pain points generic checklists don't

## Where It Sells

Launch simultaneously on all three once built:

| Channel | Role |
|---|---|
| **Gumroad** | Free to list. Primary link shared from blog posts/Pinterest bio — warm, trusting traffic lands here first (highest margin, no marketplace fee) |
| **Etsy** | $0.20/listing. Left to its own search discovery — captures cold strangers who'd never find the blog otherwise |
| **Fourthwall** | Free to list (5% fee, 0% w/ Pro). Owned-domain storefront — promoted via blog links + Pinterest bio, not left to passive discovery (it has none) |

## Sequencing

**Do not build yet.** Per the blog's own roadmap, digital products are Phase 4 — after Pinterest traffic is actually flowing (posting started 2026-07-20). Building now, before any visitor exists to test it on, is premature.

**When ready:** pre-sell the concept first (post about it, gauge real engagement/DMs) before committing to the full build — stronger validation than assuming the Etsy category proof transfers directly to this specific product.
